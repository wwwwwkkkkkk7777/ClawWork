# Code of Conduct

ClawWork is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and make technical decisions transparent.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant, depending on impact and
pattern. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and avoid
unnecessary disclosure about affected people.

For a confidential report, use the repository's
[private security advisory form](https://github.com/wwwwwkkkkkk7777/ClawWork/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`. Reports are reviewed by the
maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Good-faith reports will
not result in retaliation.

This policy applies to all ClawWork community spaces and to public
representation of the project.
